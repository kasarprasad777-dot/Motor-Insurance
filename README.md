## Problems Faced by Insurance Companies

• Insurance companies cannot easily identify which customers will make a claim in the future.

• Giving policies to high-risk customers can cause financial losses.

• Traditional methods are time-consuming and not very accurate.

• It is difficult to analyze large amounts of customer and vehicle data manually.

• Because of this, companies face problems in risk management and policy pricing.

## Purpose of the project
The purpose of this project is to predict whether a motor insurance customer will make a claim or not using machine learning techniques. By analyzing customer and vehicle information, the model helps insurance companies identify high-risk customers and make better decisions in policy pricing and risk management. This improves efficiency and reduces financial losses for the insurance company.
